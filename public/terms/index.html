6. Google Calendar

  • Connecting Google Calendar is optional. A practice owner or administrator connects it by signing in with the Google account whose calendar should mirror a diary, and must have permission to use that account for this purpose.
  • Once connected, Optometrics creates, updates and deletes appointment events in that calendar, and reads its events to keep the diary in step. Event details are limited to the patient's first name, surname initial, appointment type, time and a link back to Optometrics.
  • The practice remains responsible for who has access to the connected Google account, because anyone with access to that calendar can see these events.
  • The practice can disconnect at any time under Settings → Google Calendar, or from the Google account's security settings. Disconnecting stops the sync and deletes our stored access straight away. Events already in Google stay there until deleted in Google.
